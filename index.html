Support Privacy Policy

Contact FIIT

For help with FIIT Fitness on iPhone, email management@royceempirepresents.com. Tell us what happened and include your FIIT and iOS versions if you know them. Keep passwords and verification codes out of the message.

Sign-in and password reset

Open FIIT and choose Sign In. If you forgot your password, use Forgot password? and open the reset link sent to your account email.

Account deletion and privacy

In FIIT, open Profile and choose Delete account. For help accessing or deleting your data, contact FIIT support. To change your AI sharing choice, open Profile → Privacy & data. Read the FIIT Privacy Policy for details.
