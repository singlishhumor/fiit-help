SupportPrivacy Policy

Who we are and how to contact us

FIIT is a fitness and nutrition app. Questions about this policy, your data, or requests to access or delete it can be sent to management@royceempirepresents.com.

Information processed

We process account identifiers and email, your fitness assessment (such as age, height, weight, goals, activity and training preferences), food and water logs, exercise history and progress. If you choose camera or photo features, images may be processed to recognize foods, menus or Coach attachments. On iPhone, FIIT requires on-device speech recognition for Coach dictation. Technical requests and security information may be processed to authenticate you, prevent abuse and investigate failures.

How the information is used

Your account information and logs support tracking, history, personalized plan displays and account security. Where an AI feature is enabled, selected assessment details, food and workout information, Coach text and optional photos may be used to generate a plan, recipe or AI response. We do not require AI sharing for manual tracking, and do not intentionally send your password or raw Apple Health measurements to the AI model.

App Store subscriptions

Apple processes App Store payments. FIIT receives and stores subscription product and transaction identifiers, purchase and expiry dates, and subscription status linked to your FIIT account to verify and restore access. FIIT does not receive your Apple payment card details or use subscription records for tracking.

Third-party AI sharing and your permission

Before FIIT sends your personal fitness context, text or photos to a third-party AI model, we ask for explicit permission. FIIT uses the Qwen AI model through OpenRouter to process these requests. You can decline and use non-AI features, or withdraw your permission in Profile → Privacy & data → AI sharing permission. Withdrawal prevents new AI-sharing requests but does not erase content already processed by a provider or saved in FIIT. Provider retention and processing practices may differ.

Other providers and device features

FIIT uses Supabase for authentication, database and storage. Exercise references can come from RepDB/ExerciseDB, and product and food lookup from Open Food Facts and USDA FoodData Central. If welcome emails are enabled, FIIT uses Resend to deliver them, sharing the recipient email address and welcome-email content for delivery. Cloudflare Turnstile helps prevent abuse during sign-up, sign-in, password reset and verification-email resend requests. On iPhone, optional Apple Health access is read-only and requires your device permission. FIIT reads Apple Health measurements on the device without uploading them. Health details you enter yourself, such as food allergies, are part of your saved fitness assessment.

Storage, retention and deletion

FIIT stores account and activity records to provide your history. Coach messages and generated plans may remain in your account until removed or the account is deleted. You can start account deletion within Profile. Deletion removes the sign-in account and associated records subject to technical cleanup and any applicable legal retention obligations. Withdrawal of AI permission is separate from account deletion. The in-app Copy recent activity action copies only the last seven days of loaded food and hydration entries and recently loaded weight and workout records as JSON to your clipboard. It does not include all account history, plans, Coach messages or photos and is not a complete account export. Contact us at the address above to request access to additional data.

Permissions, choices and your rights

You can choose whether to grant photo, camera, microphone, notification and Apple Health permissions. You can manage AI permission at any time and review or delete your account in FIIT. Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to obtain, correct, or request deletion of personal data; email the contact above to ask for assistance.

Data security

The app uses encrypted network connections, authenticated requests and row-level security controls intended to restrict access to account information. No system can promise absolute security.

Adults and policy updates

FIIT is for adults aged 18 and older. It is not intended to provide individualized dieting or calorie plans to minors. We may update this policy when features or processing practices change. Review the date above for the current in-app version.
